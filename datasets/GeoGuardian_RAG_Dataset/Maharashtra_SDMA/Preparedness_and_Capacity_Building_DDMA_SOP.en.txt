Introduction: As per the provision of Disaster Management Act, 2005 Hon. Maharashtra State Disaster Management Authority has been established under the Chairmanship of the Chief Minister - The main responsibility of the Authority is to empower disaster response systems. Also, the responsibilities of the State Executive Committee are mentioned in Section 22 and 24 of this Act. In this, the remedial plans and tasks to be done to face the disaster effectively have been mentioned. Similarly, in a letter dated November 19, 2020 from the Union Ministry of Home Affairs, instructions have been given to reserve a separate fund from the State Disaster Response Fund under the name of State Disaster Risk Management Fund (SDRMF) for proper response to disaster. According to the above context, the issue of determining the standard procedure regarding the planning and expenditure limits regarding the expenditure of the funds distributed to the districts for taking various measures under disaster management was under the consideration of the government.
Government decision

Funds are distributed to all districts through the State Government under the Head of Disaster Management, Preparedness and Capacity Building (Finance Commission 2245 4188). According to Section 31 of the Disaster Management Act, 2005, the districts are provided with funds for preparing the district disaster management plan and reducing the risks and damages caused by the disaster, taking preventive measures, mitigating measures, implementing preparedness and capacity building activities, training and creating public awareness, search and rescue work, purchase/repair of materials related to disaster management. 2 The government approves the appropriation of the said fund for the following matters related to disaster management. Expenditure limit on the said items shall remain as shown in column no.-30.
The District Disaster Management Authority can spend on the following items from the funds received under account head 2245 4188. 2 A Training and Capacity Building Training 2 Workshops 3 Color Rehearsal 4 Awareness Program Printing and dissemination of awareness materials / informative materials / educational materials (IEC) on disaster management
