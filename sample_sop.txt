Rule 4.2: All hospitals must prepare emergency beds equivalent to 40% of total capacity during flood events.

Section 1: Evacuation Procedures
- When river level exceeds 1.8 meters, evacuation must begin immediately.
- Deploy at least 5 rescue boats per affected district.
- Responders must wear waterproof gear and helmets.
- Close Sangang Bridge and Krishna River Bridge if structural damage detected.
- Required safety equipment: life vests, flood barriers, portable pumps.
- First step: Issue evacuation alert via emergency broadcast system.
- Hospitals must prepare emergency beds and stock essential medicines.

Additional Rules:
- Rule 4.1: Activate community shelters within 2 km radius.
- Rule 5.0: Coordinate with local NGOs for resource distribution.
